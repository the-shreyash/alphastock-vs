# StockAssist AI

## Vision

StockAssist AI is an AI-powered trading operating system built for the Indian stock market.

The platform combines artificial intelligence, live market data, chart analysis, portfolio management, broker integration, paper trading, backtesting, news intelligence, and educational guidance into one seamless experience.

The goal is not to replace traders.

The goal is to become the smartest AI trading partner.

---

## Target Users

- Beginner traders
- Swing traders
- Intraday traders
- Long-term investors
- Students learning trading
- Professional traders

---

## Core Principles

Every AI recommendation must explain:

- Why the stock was selected
- Confidence score
- Technical reasons
- Fundamental reasons
- Risk
- Reward
- News impact
- Sector performance
- Market conditions

The AI should educate users rather than simply giving buy/sell signals.
